Question 3

Explain the process of photosynthesis and its importance to the Earth's ecosystem.

Model Answer / Key

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants and some other organisms use sunlight to synthesize foods from carbon dioxide and water. Photosynthesis in plants generally involves the green pigment chlorophyll and generates oxygen as a byproduct.

Student Response

Photosynthesis is how plants make their food. They take in sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide from the air. The chlorophyll in their leaves helps them do this. When they make the food, they also release oxygen into the air, which is very important because humans and animals need oxygen to breathe. Without photosynthesis, the ecosystem would collapse because there would be no food base and no oxygen for other organisms.
